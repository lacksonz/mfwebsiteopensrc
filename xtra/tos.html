3. acceptable use

don't use xtra to:

  • break discord's own terms of service or community guidelines
  • harass, threaten, or target real people, including through the joke or roleplay commands
  • attempt to overload, spam, or abuse the bot's commands, api, or dashboard
  • exploit a bug for personal gain or to disrupt other servers
  • circumvent a blacklist, a jail, or another server's own moderation setup
  • use generated ai content (text, images, audio) to impersonate a real person in a way intended to deceive, defraud, or harass
the /impersonate command: writes a short, clearly-labelled fake message in someone's texting style, for a laugh among people who already know each other. it is not for impersonating someone outside that context, and every message it produces is labelled as generated.

4. moderation features

xtra provides real moderation tools — kicks, bans, timeouts, warnings, a jail system, and automod — that act with the discord permissions your server has granted the bot. these are provided as-is. you're responsible for configuring them appropriately for your server, granting the bot only the permissions it needs, and reviewing its actions.

the joke commands (where present) are cosmetic and perform no real moderation action, regardless of their name.

a jail configuration strips and later restores a member's roles. we make a reasonable effort to store and return those roles correctly, but if a role is deleted, or moved above the bot's own role, while someone is jailed, it may not be restorable, and that's on discord's permission model rather than something we can bypass.

daily usage limits apply to ai features and reset at midnight utc. we may adjust these limits at any time without notice to keep the service sustainable for everyone.

voice features join a channel only when a command is run and do not otherwise process voice audio. see the privacy policy for more.
